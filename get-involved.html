Become a helper

An hour every two weeks is plenty. You choose how far across the DFW area you'll travel and what you're happy to do, and you can say no to any request without giving a reason. We'll ask for two references and run a background check where the help involves driving or regular visits — we pay for it, and we walk you through it.

What happens after you press send

No automated queue, no reference number to quote. Here's the whole of it.

  • 1. We read it that day

    Both forms land on the coordinator's desk, not in a shared inbox nobody owns. Requests marked urgent get looked at first.

  • 2. We call you back within two business days

    A short, friendly call to check we've understood. For requests, we'll agree a time. For helpers, we'll talk through what you fancy doing and start the references.

  • 3. We make the match

    We look for someone close by who's free when you need them. You get their first name before they arrive, and they carry a Neighbourly Hands card.

  • 4. We check in afterwards

    A quick call or text to ask how it went. If the pairing worked, we'll keep you together. If it didn't, say so — we'll try someone else, no awkwardness.

Other ways to pitch in

Not everyone has a free hour on a weekday, and not every useful thing is a visit to someone's door.

  • The Tuesday drop-in

    Every Tuesday, 10am to noon, at The Old Dairy. Tea, cookies, and someone to sit with while you sort out a letter or just ask a question. Nobody signs anything. Come once or come weekly.

  • Donate a vehicle hour

    If you have a car, a van or a cargo bike and an hour spare, we'll match it to a lift or a bulky pick-up anywhere in the DFW area. You tell us the hour; we fill it or we don't bother you. Fuel costs reimbursed at 45p a mile.

  • Host a street sign-up

    Put the kettle on, open your front room or your front yard for an hour, and we'll bring the forms and a coordinator. Most streets in Plano and Frisco come away with four or five new helpers.

  • Pass the number on

    The quietest and most useful thing you can do. Give 0117 496 0188 to the neighbor who'd never call us themselves. We'll take it gently from there.
